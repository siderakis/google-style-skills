# Attribution

The skills in this repository are derived from two of Google's published
guides. Both are Creative Commons licensed, and both are credited here as
those licenses require.

## Google developer documentation style guide

Source: <https://developers.google.com/style>
License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Retrieved: 2026-09-13

Derived into: `skills/google-style`, `skills/google-style-grammar`,
`skills/google-style-punctuation`, `skills/google-style-structure`,
`skills/google-style-formatting`, `skills/google-style-code`,
`skills/google-style-inclusive`, `skills/google-style-review`.

`skills/google-style/references/word-list.md` is a near-verbatim copy of
<https://developers.google.com/style/word-list>. Relative links were made
absolute and the per-entry anchor icons were removed; the wording is
unchanged.

The eight SKILL.md files are a rewrite, not a copy: the guide's roughly 60
pages were reorganized into eight task-shaped documents, and the prose was
rewritten to address an agent rather than a human writer. Rules, verdicts, and
the Recommended / Not recommended example pairs are Google's.

## Google engineering practices documentation

Source: <https://github.com/google/eng-practices>
License: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
Retrieved: 2026-09-13

Derived into: `skills/google-code-review` (from the Code Review Developer
Guide: the standard, what to look for, navigating a CL, speed, writing
comments, handling pushback, and emergencies) and `skills/google-cl-author`
(from the CL Author's Guide: small CLs, CL descriptions, and handling reviewer
comments).

Google writes _CL_ for "changelist"; the skills note that this reads as commit,
pull request, or branch elsewhere. Section structure and the quoted examples
are Google's.

## Relationship to Google

This is an unofficial adaptation. It is not produced, endorsed, or reviewed by
Google. Google's guides are the authority; where this repository and a Google
guide disagree, the Google guide is correct — please open an issue.
